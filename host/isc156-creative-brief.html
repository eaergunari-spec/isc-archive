Your edition, your imprint.

Congratulations once again on winning ISC 155 with Csinszka — “Felsütött a napsugára.” Competition rules and administration remain with ISC, but STJOHN now has a visible creative role in shaping the atmosphere, visual identity and opening message of ISC 156. Short answers are perfectly fine.

Your host message

What does hosting ISC 156 mean to STJOHN after winning ISC 155? Please write approximately 2–4 sentences. This may be quoted directly in the official launch announcement.

Welcome to ISC 156

Complete this sentence in your own way: “STJOHN welcomes the delegations of ISC 156 to an edition that will be…”

The mood

Choose 3–5 words that you would like ISC 156 to feel like.

Examples: elegant · warm · euphoric · nostalgic · nocturnal · futuristic · romantic · playful · cinematic · mysterious · colourful · intimate · glamorous · surreal

Colour world

What colours do you associate with your vision for ISC 156? Exact colour codes are not necessary.

Examples: midnight blue + gold · deep red + cream · silver + electric blue · sunset orange + violet

A host symbol

If STJOHN could leave one recurring visual symbol or motif throughout ISC 156, what would it be? It may be cultural, natural, architectural or completely abstract.

The motto

What should be the central phrase or motto of ISC 156? One final motto is enough, or you may send up to three alternatives.

The host’s welcome song

Choose one existing song that would be the perfect unofficial welcome song for ISC 156. It will not compete; it may inspire the launch atmosphere, host playlist or opening editorial.

What do you want to hear?

As host nation, what would you personally love to see more of in ISC 156? This is not a rule; delegations remain completely free in their selections.

Native languages · experimental entries · big pop songs · alternative music · strong storytelling · local cultural influences · unexpected artists · riskier selections

A message to the delegations

Imagine all delegations are standing on stage on the first night of ISC 156. What would you like to say to them?

Your ISC 155 memory

Apart from your own winning entry, what was your favourite memory from ISC 155? It can be another song, an editorial, a voting moment, a surprise result, a delegation or simply something you enjoyed.

The night you won

When you realised STJOHN had won ISC 155, what was your immediate reaction? A short, personal answer is encouraged.

The host signature

Would you like ISC 156 to have a small recurring host signature? This might be a symbol, emoji, short phrase, tiny graphic idea or recurring sign-off.

Examples: ✦ · ☀ · “From STJOHN, with love.” · “Where the light begins.”
